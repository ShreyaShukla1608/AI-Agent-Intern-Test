# Return & Refund Policy (Current Active Policy)

Aster & Row offers a **30-day return window** from the date of delivery for all eligible items.

## Return Conditions
* Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached.
* Final sale items, gift cards, and personalized products are non-refundable.

## Refund Process
* Once received and inspected, refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
* Store credit options are available for exchanges.
